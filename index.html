What is Hack Club, anyway?

A worldwide club run entirely by teenagers.

Hack Club started as one high school coding club and grew into a global network of student-led clubs — thousands of teenagers across dozens of countries, all building software and hardware outside of class, all connected on the same Slack.

There's no curriculum. Instead, Hack Club runs YSWS programs — "You Ship, We Ship" — where the deal is simple: finish a real project, and Hack Club sends you hardware, funding, or a trip to go build even more. It rewards shipping, not attendance.

LTC Hack Club is our local chapter — same network, same programs, same energy, just meeting down the hall instead of across the world.

What we actually do

Less talking, more building.

Ship Nights

Weekly meetups where you just... build. Websites, games, bots, hardware — you pick, we're just the room and the wifi.

YSWS Programs

Tap into Hack Club's global "You Ship, We Ship" programs — finish something real and the network sends you stuff for it.

⚡

Workshops

Quick hands-on sessions on whatever's useful — Git, your first website, Arduino, or whatever someone in the club wants to teach.

Show & Tell

Demo whatever you shipped, however unfinished. Half-broken projects get just as much applause as polished ones.
